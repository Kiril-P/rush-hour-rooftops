RUSH HOUR ROOFTOPS

Serve this folder with any static HTTP server. For example:

  python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1

Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 in a desktop browser.

Drag from a small home's bright terminal to a matching rooftop station.
Diagonals work. Garden islands stay clear. The opening waits for you.
Space pauses and allows building. 1 / 2 selects speed.
Express: click two endpoints to bypass junctions.
Interchange: upgrade a junction with three or more approaches.
Click a roof to inspect its routes and cabin availability.
Right-click erases. E selects sweep erasing. I selects Inspect.
Escape cancels, closes inspection, or opens the city menu.
Scroll or + / - zooms. Alt-drag or arrows pan. F fits the city.
Choose supplies each week. New stations have 60 seconds of setup grace.
Clear station overload within 30 seconds. The ? button explains all tools.

The city menu includes restart and new city. After failure, inspect the network.
Music, ambience and effects levels are in the city menu. Sound starts on your first click.
All graphics, fonts, and audio work locally. Font licenses are in licenses/.
